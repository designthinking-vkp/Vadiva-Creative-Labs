Engineering Product Sprint

Transforming School Projects into Venture-Grade Products

Most student inventions remain stuck at cardboard models or science fair posters. 5 Days, 5 Things You'll Make

The 5-Day Forge Journey

An aggressive, milestone-driven curriculum engineered to mirror real-world Silicon Valley and hardware incubators:

01

DAY 1

Team Charter + Prototype Audit

Assemble engineering crews, dissect current prototype flaws, stress-test mechanical load, and map failure points.

02

DAY 2

Problem Card + Redesign Plan

Frame user pain points, draft engineering CAD schematics, source precision electronics, and plan Version 2.0 architecture.

03

DAY 3

Working V2 + Cost Sheet

Fabricate physical Version 2.0 build with laser-cut chassis, 3D printed fittings, and calculate unit Bill of Materials (BOM).

04

DAY 4

Test Report, Brand Kit, Pitch

Field-test operational reliability, establish product identity & logo, and rehearse venture pitch decks with jury coaches.

05

DAY 5

Demo Day, Final Product + Dossier

Live public showcase before industry evaluators. Defend functioning hardware builds, unit economics, and handover dossiers.

Candidate Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the ₹6,000 fee? +
The ₹6,000 fee covers the complete 5 days and 4 nights: air-conditioned accommodation at TVIS Ponneri, all meals and snacks, hardware lab consumable materials (3D filaments, sensors, acrylic, fasteners), mentor sessions, and demo day participation.
Can individual students apply without a pre-formed team? +
Absolutely! Under Track 2 ("Curious Ones"), individual applicants are evaluated and matched during Day 1 Team Charter with complementary peers based on skills and problem interests.
What safety and supervision measures are in place? +
TVIS Ponneri is an enclosed, gated residential campus with 24/7 biometric security, resident infirmary and medical nurse on call, separate boys and girls hostel wings with dedicated floor wardens, and strict mentor supervision.
How does the StartupTN pathway work? +
Evaluators during Day 5 Demo Day select high-potential projects demonstrating verified product function and viable unit economics. Top teams are nominated to present directly at the official StartupTN celebration in January 2027.
