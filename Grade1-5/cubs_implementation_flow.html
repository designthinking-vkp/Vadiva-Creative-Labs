Year 2+ — Stable Progression Model Ongoing
From Year 2, the school follows the standard Bharat Scouts & Guides annual progression. Each grade advances one level per year.
Stable Model Active: New Grade 1 students begin Pravesh each year. Existing students advance one badge level per year. Grade 5 students work toward Chaturth Charan and Golden Arrow preparation.

The 🌿 Inspired Living programme runs alongside every lesson, adding monthly practical life-skills that reinforce Scout values of self-reliance, service, and care.
Inspired Living — Life Skills Programme Grades 1–5
Practical life-skills curriculum integrated monthly across all grades — cooking, gardening, household management, repairs, and cultural heritage.
Integration Note: Inspired Living runs alongside the Cubs & Bulbuls programme. Every month, students develop a practical life skill that complements their Scout values — self-reliance, service, and care for living things.

🔗 How Inspired Living Connects to Cubs Values
🌱
Gardening → Care for Living Things
Daily watering builds the Scout habit of regular Good Turns and responsibility for something beyond oneself.
🍽
Cooking → Service & Teamwork
Preparing food for others is a form of service — directly aligned to the Scout value of the Good Turn.
🏠
Household Chores → Discipline & Responsibility
Consistent chores build the self-discipline that the Cub Law requires: clean in thought, word, and deed.
🎉
Thematic Activities → Heritage & Citizenship
Pongal, Navaratri, Diwali activities fulfil the Scout duty to country and cultivate cultural pride.
🔧
Home Maintenance → Be Prepared
Knowing how to fix, repair, and maintain things is the physical expression of the Scout motto: Be Prepared.
