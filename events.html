1. Flagship Tech & Design Festivals

Annual Showcase

Vadiva Tech & Design Fest

Our premier multi-day innovation convergence platform draws over 5,000+ students and features specialized event horizons where textbooks turn into hardware engineering realities.

The Competitive Arenas

High-velocity drone racing competitions, multi-terrain remote-controlled vehicle (RC multi-terrain vehicle) tracks, customized radio-controlled aviation flight lines (RC planes competition), and soccer-based robotics arenas (Robo Football/Soccer).

Knowledge & Workshop Tracks

Operating up to 25 technical workshops, 10+ masterclasses, and specialized tech talks and panel debates running simultaneously across campus venues.

Protospark Product Pitch Arena

The definitive entrepreneurial final evaluated by an industry jury. Students face tough industry panels to defend their working builds. Finalists have previously fabricated physical mud ovens on site to demonstrate thermodynamic control; the pitch track clears over ₹25,00,000+ in pure prize money and startup grants to scale youth ventures.
