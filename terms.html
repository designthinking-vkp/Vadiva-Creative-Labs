Guidelines and terms governing information submission, contact requests, and data usage.

Welcome to Vadiva Creative Labs. These Terms and Conditions outline the rules, privacy protocols, and consent guidelines for users who submit information via our contact and partnership forms.

1. Information Collection & Scope

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, registration form, or partner consultation fields, we collect details including your name, email address, institutional affiliation (if applicable), and message content. This data is strictly used to evaluate and fulfill your strategic requests.

2. Consent to Communication

By submitting a form on this website, you explicitly grant Vadiva Creative Labs permission to reach out to you using the email address and contact details provided. This communication is limited to addressing your inquiry, setting schedules, proposing lab audits, or sharing school partnerships.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We take data security seriously. All form submissions are protected using standard encryption and database security systems. We do not sell, distribute, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party marketing companies, brokers, or external entities.

You agree to use our forms for legitimate inquiries, registrations, and partnership consultations only. You are prohibited from submitting spam, commercial advertisements, malicious links, or offensive content through our communication channels.

5. Data Access & Deletion Rights

You retain complete ownership over your personal data. At any time, you can request that we update, correct, or completely delete your contact information and historical records from our outreach database by sending an email to reach@vadivacreativelabs.com.

We reserve the right to modify these terms and conditions to reflect adjustments in security protocols or local regulations. Changes will be reflected directly on this page with the updated revision timestamp.
