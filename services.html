We completely transform standard rooms into active, hands-on fabrication environments. MakerForge is our proprietary physical laboratory model supporting multi-disciplinary learning. We oversee spacing layouts, hardware provisioning (electronics benches, rapid prototyping stations), and implement strict safety compliance systems.

Optimized ergonomic spacing layouts custom-engineered for institutions.
Industrial-grade hardware provisioning (Electronics, 3D printers, woodworking).
