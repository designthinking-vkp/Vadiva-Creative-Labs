The Narrative Journey

The Island Survival Mission

When our ship runs aground on an uninhabited cove, the crew has no internet, no smartphones, and no outside help. Over three intense build days, participants form naval crews to harvest resources, apply physics, master woodworking, engineer electronics, and fabricate a full-scale seaworthy vessel.

Phase 01

Island Survey & Scavenge

Establish basecamp, learn navigation and celestial orientation, map timber supplies, and begin buoyancy displacement calculations.

Phase 02

Hull Engineering & Rigging

Saw timber frames, stitch canvas sails, 3D print waterproof rudder hinges, laser-cut helm wheels, and wire emergency electronic bilge pumps.

Phase 03

The Great Lake Regatta

Launch day! Crews board their constructed vessels, test buoyancy under real water load, navigate the lagoon course, and celebrate survival.

Hands-On Engineering

YOU'LL SAW, STITCH, PRINT, CODE AND CREATE

Eight immersive maker stations empowering young sailors to fabricate every inch of their escape vessel:

01

Carpentry

Measure, saw, drill, and fasten timber structural ribs, planking, and reinforced keel frames using professional hand tools.

02

Sewing

Cut canvas fabric, stitch heavy-duty sail panels, lash maritime grommets, and master nautical cleat and clove-hitch knots.

03

3D Printing

CAD model and additive-print custom watertight deck brackets, line pulleys, rudder mounts, and mast clamps.

04

Laser & CNC

Precision-cut interlocking plywood bulkheads, decorative ship wheel spokes, compass housings, and floor grating.

05

Robotics

Wire motorized bilge water pumps, configure automated distress beacons, and script sensor-triggered orientation signals.

06

STEM Lab

Test Archimedes principle, calibrate center of mass versus metacenter, and calculate volume displacement to prevent capsizing.

07

Art & Heraldry

Illustrate crew pennants and flag crests, hand-paint nautical charts, and sculpt decorative wooden bow figureheads.

08

Mainland Expedition

The grand finale! Sea trials on the lake, crew rowing coordination, navigation benchmarks, and triumphant return to the mainland.

Hands-on Pedagogy

Why Storytelling Drives Deep Engineering

When children are placed inside an imaginative, high-stakes story world, math and physics cease to be abstract textbook formulas. Friction, buoyancy, structural load, and electrical continuity become urgent, practical survival tools.

  • ✓ Collaborative Crew Structure: Roles rotate between skipper, rigger, carpenter, and navigator.
  • ✓ Resilience & Troubleshooting: Re-engineering leaks and buoyancy imbalances in real time.
  • ✓ Unmatched Pride of Build: Boarding a boat that you designed, sawed, stitched, and wired yourself.

Frequently Answered

Camp Questions & Answers

Does my child need to know swimming? +
Swimming is not mandatory! All sea trials take place in calm, shallow waters with mandatory certified personal flotation devices (life jackets) fitted properly to every child. Certified lifeguards accompany all water activities.
What should students pack for the 5-day camp? +
Comfortable cotton clothes, closed-toe workshop shoes, nightwear, personal toiletries, and any prescription medications. All tools, building materials, stationery, and safety gear are fully provided.
Can friends or siblings be placed in the same boat crew? +
Yes! During Day 1 crew assembly, participants can indicate their preferred crewmates, and our lead mentors will ensure friends work together on the same build.
What does the ₹6,000 fee cover? +
The all-inclusive fee covers full 5 days and 4 nights accommodation at TVIS Ponneri, all meals, workshop lumber and mechanical components, 3D printing filaments, safety equipment, camp merchandise, and graduation certificates.
