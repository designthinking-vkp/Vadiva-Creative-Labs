VELAMMAL BODHI CAMPUS

Staff Calculation & Financial forecasting System Guide

Document Version: 2.0 (June 2026)
Target System: CBSE Day School Planner

1. Introduction & Overview

This Decision Support System (DSS) is designed specifically for Velammal Bodhi Campus administrators to model, project, and optimize manpower and budgets for a new CBSE Day School over its first five years of operation. The system automates the translation of grade-wise student enrollments and period allocations into required staffing levels, monthly/annual payrolls, teacher-to-student ratios, and unused teacher capacity room.

Affiliation Compliance: The model integrates standard CBSE affiliation rules to ensure baseline staffing requirements (e.g. mandatory Principal, Stage Academic Coordinators, Librarian, PET, and Homeroom teachers) are met, even if enrollment is low.

2. Setup Wizard & Cohort Growth Projection

On initial load, a full-screen onboarding wizard captures the Year 1 student strength and location parameters. Projections for Years 2-5 are calculated automatically using cohort progression rules:

  • Entry-Level Growth: Kindergarten classes (Pre-KG, LKG, UKG) grow at the specified annual growth rate.
  • Cohort Slide: Students in Grade N slide up to Grade N+1 in the subsequent projection year.
  • Grade Expansion Schedule:
    • Year 1: Pre-KG to Grade 8 are open. Secondary and Senior Secondary classes are closed.
    • Year 2: Grades 9 and 11 open automatically. Special programs (Foundation, IIT, NEET) commence.
    • Year 3: Grades 10 and 12 open automatically. The school becomes fully operational (Pre-KG to Grade 12).
    • Years 4 & 5: Stabilization and capacity growth years.

3. Core CBSE Staffing Rules

Homeroom Teacher Rules

  • KG Stage (PKG, LKG, UKG): Required teachers are calculated solely on a homeroom section basis (no subject specialists). Standard allocation is 1 Homeroom teacher per section.
  • Grades 1 & 2: 1 Homeroom teacher per section handles Mathematics, EVS, General Knowledge, and Co-curricular slots. Pooled teachers instruct English and Tamil.

Subject Teacher Pooling

Workloads for core academic subjects (English, Tamil, Hindi, Mathematics, Social Science, Computer/AI, and Science) are pooled at the department level. The required staffing count is calculated by dividing total periods needed across stages by stage capacities:

Academic Stage Standard Teacher Workload Capacity
Primary & Middle Stages (Grades 1-8) 32 Periods per Week
Secondary & Senior Secondary (Grades 9-12) 36 Periods per Week

Science Stage Options

  • Option A (Composite): Middle School Science periods are split between two composite roles to optimize utilization:
    • Science Teacher 1: Instructs Physics and Chemistry.
    • Science Teacher 2: Instructs Biology and Physics.
  • Option B (Specialists): Physics, Chemistry, and Biology are treated as separate departments across all stages, hiring independent specialists.

4. Special Academic Programs (IIT & NEET)

Rather than hiring general "Program Faculty", the system splits IIT and NEET workloads among subject specialists.

  • IIT Programme Specialists: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and an optional Biology specialist (enable/disable via settings).
  • NEET Programme Specialists: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Option 1: Combined Biology; Option 2: Separate Botany and Zoology).
  • Faculty Sharing Mode:
    • Shared Faculty: Program periods are added directly to CBSE teacher workloads to optimize utilization. Payroll is distributed proportionally based on FTE teaching fractions.
    • Dedicated Faculty: Recruited separately in independent program pools.

5. Export & Custom Report Designing

Using the Custom Report Designer tab, users can:

  1. Drag and drop desired report blocks from the available sections.
  2. Re-order pages using sorting controls in the layout panel.
  3. Apply filters (Year, Program, Category) and toggle branding headers, logo crests, and page numbers.
  4. Export the compiled report packages directly to **Print/PDF**, **Microsoft Excel (table sheets)**, or **Microsoft Word**.
