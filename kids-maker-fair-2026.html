Experiential Learning Arena

Where Young Curious Minds Become Real Creators

At Vadiva Creative Labs, we believe children learn best when their hands are on the tools. The Kids Maker Fair & Repair Mela is an immersive celebration where young inventors discover that things around them aren't black boxes — they are assembled from parts that can be understood, repaired, reimagined, and rebuilt.

Pillar 1

MAKE IT

Raw materials become working mechanisms. Kids cut, sand, fasten, and assemble their own tactile projects using child-safe maker tools.

Pillar 2

FIX IT

Learn to diagnose why things stop working! In our Repair Mela station, children unscrew broken toys and everyday gadgets to discover gears, wires, and fixes.

Pillar 3

REUSE IT

Transform discarded cardboard, bottles, and scrap components into creative automata and kinetic contraptions. Sustainability meets engineering innovation!

Hands-On Stations

Funfilled Activity Arenas

Five immersive activity stations guided by experienced makers and engineers:

Woodcraft & Hand Tools

Practice using real child-friendly hand tools: miniature hammers, screwdrivers, sandpaper, and brackets to craft a personalized keepsake.

Toy Surgery & Teardown Lab

Ever wondered what's inside a remote-controlled car or electric fan? Deconstruct and inspect circuits, motor gears, and wiring safely.

Electronics & Circuit Prototyping

Snap together conductive circuits, light up LEDs, wire simple switches, and discover the fundamentals of clean electrical currents.

Upcycled Kinetic Automata

Harness cams, linkages, and recycled materials to build a moving automata sculpture that waves, spins, or jumps when cranked.

Creative Robotics & Bristlebots

Construct buzzing micro-robots powered by coin cells and vibration motors, competing in a friendly maze obstacle sprint.

Skills That Last a Lifetime

Maker Skills Kids Take Home

  • ✓

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the Maker Fair? +
Any child aged between 6 and 16 years with a curiosity for making, building, or fixing things! No prior workshop experience is required. Activities are tailored to different age groups.
Can parents accompany their children? +
Yes! Parents are welcome to attend, observe, and cheer their children on. We also have a comfortable parents lounge with seating and refreshments.
Are the tools and workshops safe for young children? +
Safety is our absolute highest priority. All workstations are guided by certified educators and makers. Hand tools are calibrated for young learners, and safety goggles and gear are provided where necessary.
